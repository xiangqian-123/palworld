## Purchase & Platforms

### 1. How much does Palworld cost?

$29.99. Steam limited-time 25% off ≈ $22.49. The 1.0 full release keeps the price.

See [Download & Platforms Overview](/en/guide/download)

### 2. Is it on Xbox Game Pass?

Yes. Palworld is playable via PC Game Pass and Xbox Game Pass Ultimate.

See [Download & Platforms Overview](/en/guide/download)

### 3. Can I play on PS5?

Yes. Palworld is sold on the PlayStation Store for $29.99. PS4 is not supported.

See [PS5 Version Details](/en/guide/ps5)

### 4. Is there a mobile version?

No native mobile version yet. Palworld Mobile (by Krafton) and Palworld Online (by Garena) are planned for 2026. For now you can play on mobile via Xbox Cloud or Steam Link.

See [Mobile Version Details](/en/guide/mobile)

### 5. Is there a Nintendo Switch version?

No. Due to the Nintendo patent dispute, a Switch release is highly unlikely.

See [Version Update History](/en/guide/updates)

### 6. Is there cross-platform multiplayer?

Yes. v0.5.0 added cross-platform multiplayer across PC / PS5 / Xbox / Mac. The host enables Cross-Play in World Settings.

See [Download & Platforms Overview](/en/guide/download)

## Breeding & Pals

### 7. How do I breed Pals?

You need the Breeding Farm unlocked at level 19, place 1 male + 1 female Pal + a Cake. See [Breeding Guide](/en/guide/breeding).

### 8. Do Pals evolve?

No. Palworld has no evolution system. Variants are independent species that must be obtained separately.

See [Breeding Guide](/en/guide/breeding)

### 9. How many Pals are there?

The 1.0 full release has 288 in total, of which 72 are new in 1.0. See [Pal Codex](/en/guide/pals) and [1.0 New Pals](/en/guide/new-pals).

### 10. What's the best combat Pal?

Orserk (Electric/Dragon) is top-tier DPS, and Jetragon is a legendary flying combat Pal. See [Pal Codex](/en/guide/pals).

### 11. Where's the breeding calculator?

[Breeding Calculator](/en/breeding-calculator) — both forward and reverse lookup. See [Breeding Guide](/en/guide/breeding).

## Servers & Multiplayer

### 12. How do I set up a dedicated server?

On PC use the Steam tool to install Palworld Dedicated Server, up to 32 players. See [Dedicated Server Tutorial](/en/guide/server).

### 13. What's the difference between invite multiplayer and a dedicated server?

Invite multiplayer supports up to 4 players and shuts down when the host goes offline. A dedicated server supports 32 players and runs 24/7.

See [Dedicated Server Tutorial](/en/guide/server)

## Materials & Crafting

### 14. How do I get Hardwood?

Chop ancient giant trees, buy from a merchant for 100 gold, or defeat Wumpo Botan for 2–5. See [Materials Codex](/en/guide/materials).

### 15. Where's Chromite?

There's no ore node on the map. Synthesize via the Ancient Material Synthesizer (8,000 work/unit), or kill Silvegis for 2–3.

See [Materials Codex](/en/guide/materials)

### 16. How do I get Coralum Ore?

Via salvaging sunken ships (the Salvage system added in v0.6.0) or oil platform chests. See [Materials Codex](/en/guide/materials) and [Terraria Crossover](/en/guide/terraria).

### 17. Where's Crude Oil?

Large offshore oil platform chests, 10–300 per opening. See [Materials Codex](/en/guide/materials).

### 18. How do I get Ancient Civilization Core?

Drops from boss battles, used for ultimate tech like the AI Core. See [Materials Codex](/en/guide/materials).

## Terraria Crossover

### 19. What's in the Terraria crossover?

Tides of Terraria (v0.6.0) adds 27 Pals, 3 islands, Terraria dungeons, Eye of Cthulhu / Moon Lord bosses, fishing/salvage systems and more. See [Terraria Crossover Explained](/en/guide/terraria).

### 20. Can Eye of Cthulhu be captured?

Yes. Eye of Cthulhu can be captured as a Pal after defeating it in a Terraria dungeon. See [Terraria Crossover Explained](/en/guide/terraria).

## TCG Cards

### 21. What is the Palworld TCG?

Palworld OFFICIAL CARD GAME, published by Bushiroad, a physical card game released July 30, 2026. See [TCG Card Game](/en/guide/tcg).

## Other

### 22. What's the level cap?

Level 80 (1.0 full release).

### 23. Is cross-save supported?

No. Saves are tied to the platform; the Global Palbox can only transfer Pals between the same platform under the same account.

### 24. How do I pick passives?

For combat use Legend + Musclehead + Ferocious + Brave; for workers use Workaholic + Artisan + Serious + Serenity. See [Passive Mechanics](/en/guide/passives).

### 25. What is "Palword"?

"Palword" is a common misspelling of Palworld. This site covers search demand for all spelling variants, handled on both the homepage and FAQ page.
