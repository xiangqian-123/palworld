In Palworld, gold's main use is buying rare materials and key items from merchants. Once you hit endgame, repairing gear and replenishing materials burn gold fast. This guide collects 6 community-verified money methods, ordered by difficulty "early → mid → late".

## Early: Sell Surplus Goods

### Sell Surplus Food

Base farming + cooking keeps producing large amounts of surplus food (Salad, Pizza, Bread, etc.). Once food exceeds your Pals' daily consumption, it's "cash sitting on the books."

> Community tip: the Feed Box doesn't need 100+ servings stockpiled; sell the surplus directly to merchants — the most hassle-free early income source.

### Sell Basic Materials

Wood, Stone, Fiber and other basic materials overflow massively in mid-late game; sell them in bulk to merchants to recover gold.

## Mid: Farm Alpha Pals + Oil

### Sell Alpha Pal Drops (valuable items)

Defeating or capturing Alpha Pals (fixed-respawn large Pals) drops "valuable" items. These sell high, and farming Alpha Pals also farms Legendary Schematics — **make gold and stock endgame gear at once**.

### Relaxaurus for High Quality Pal Oil

A community-acknowledged efficient gold farm: repeatedly kill Relaxaurus, which drops High Quality Pal Oil, **about 1,000 gold each**. Bulk farming is very efficient.

## Late: Endgame Income

### Sell Tower Boss Drops

Each faction tower boss drops rare materials on a fixed schedule; repeat challenges (daily reset) yield a steady stream of sellable materials.

### Sell Surplus Bred Pals

Breeding surplus high-rarity Pals can be sold to Pal merchants for gold while clearing storage.

## Quick Comparison

| Method | Unlock Stage | Efficiency | Side Benefit |
|------|---------|------|---------|
