World Tree is the main-story endpoint of the Palworld 1.0 full release. This giant tree, visible from the EA launch but inaccessible, only opened in 1.0. This guide covers the unlock process, the 8 tower bosses, the final boss, in-tree Pals and endgame materials.

## What Is the World Tree

The World Tree is a standalone endgame zone beyond the Palpagos Islands, with Pal levels **75–80**. It is sealed by 8 towers + 8 Key Spheres, and the main-story final boss is imprisoned deep within. Drops and gathering inside the World Tree are the core source for "graduation" gear and the awakening system.

## How to Unlock the World Tree

Entering the World Tree is a **two-step gate**, both required:

| Step | Requirement | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| 1. Collect all 8 Key Spheres | Defeat all 8 tower bosses | Each tower drops 1 Key Sphere on first clear; no repeat farming needed |
| 2. Catch Panthalus | 4 Echobones → craft Echoing Flute → summon and catch Panthalus at Deserted Islet | Keep Panthalus in your party and use it at the World Tree terminal |

> Common pitfall: collecting only the Key Spheres won't open the door — you must first complete the Panthalus quest (all 4 Echobones are legendary key items). Panthalus is a Lv80 Water-type, countered by Electric Pals; bring a Ring of Mercy to avoid killing it by mistake.

## Overview of the 8 Tower Bosses

1.0 cut the tower battle time limit from 10 minutes to 5 minutes, and rebalanced all bosses. Progress in level order for the smoothest run:

| Order | Tower Boss | Pal | Element | Weakness | Level | Location |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Zoe & Grizzbolt | Grizzbolt | Electric | Ground | 10 | Rayne Syndicate Tower (Windswept Hills) |
| 2 | Lily & Lyleen | Lyleen | Grass | Fire | 20 | Free Pal Alliance Tower (Crescent Moon Shore) |
| 3 | Axel & Orserk | Orserk | Electric/Dragon | Ground, Ice | 30 | Eternal Pyre Tower (Mount Obsidian) |
| 4 | Marcus & Faleris | Faleris | Fire | Water | 40 | PIDF Tower (Dessicated Desert) |
| 5 | Victor & Shadowbeak | Shadowbeak | Dark | Dragon | 50 | PAL Genetic Research Tower (Astral Mountains) |
| 6 | Saya & Selyne | Selyne | Dark | Dragon | 55 | Moonflower Tower (Sakurajima) |
| 7 | Bjorn & Bastigor | Bastigor | Ice | Fire | 60 | Feybreak Tower |
| 8 | Auri & Shaolong | Shaolong | Dragon/Water | Electric, Ice | 68 | Sunreach Tower |

The difficulty turning point is **Victor & Shadowbeak (Lv50)** — from here your gear and Pal training must keep up. The 8th tower, Auri & Shaolong, requires shutting down the Sunreach floating island's defense system (6 glowing devices) before the fight begins.

## Final Boss: Zenara & Astralym

After collecting all 8 Key Spheres, the sealed gate deep in the World Tree opens, leading to the final battle:

- **Astralym**: Lv80, **typeless** — no elemental weakness to exploit, pure damage, training and dodging
- Around 30k HP it enters a self-destruct phase, with roughly a 20-second window you must break through or the whole party wipes
- Bring a fast flying mount like Jetragon to dodge lasers
- **Astralym cannot be captured**: defeating it only records it in the Paldeck, it cannot be used as a Pal
- Clear reward: Ancient Technology Points, Dark Skill Fruit "Psycho Gravity", Zenara's hat

## Pals Inside the World Tree

The World Tree is split into the outer zone (Lv70–78) and the depths (Lv80 legacy Pal group). The most common representative Pals:

| Pal | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mimog | 70–80 | The most abundant Pal in the World Tree (700+ spawn points) |
| Grizzbolt | 70–80 | Wild form of Tower 1 boss |
| Bastigor | 75–80 | Wild form of Tower 7 boss |
| Faleris / Faleris Aqua | 76–80 | High-speed Fire flying mount |
| Selyne | 76–80 | Sakurajima tower boss form |
| Orserk | 74–80 | Top-tier power-generation Pal |
| Anubis / Shadowbeak / Astegon etc. | 80 | Depth Lv80 legacy group (~60 fixed spawns) |

The 8 Alpha bosses inside the World Tree (Dualith, Whalaska Ignis, Renjishi, Aegidron, Celesdir Noct, etc.) are a stable source of awakening and graduation materials. For the full distribution of all 100 Pals, see the [Pal Codex](/zh-CN/guide/pals) and each Pal's [location page](/zh-CN/pal/anubis/location).

## Endgame Materials

The World Tree is the source of the following core materials (see [Endgame Guide](/zh-CN/guide/endgame) for details):

- **Radiant Gem**: Core of the Awakening system; Lv80 legacy Pal group inside the World Tree drops 10–20 per kill guaranteed
- **World Tree Holy Water**: Drops from 25 new Pals, raw material for ultimate potions / Paloxite ingots
- **Ancient Relics**: 5 tiers, feed to the Ancient Relic Recycler to produce a Mythical Wood economy
- **Mythical Wood**: Ultimate gear material

## Pre-Entry Checklist

1. Reach **Lv80**, with at least 4 main Pals at graduation training level
2. Prepare **endgame armor + legendary weapons** (legendary blueprints in the Endgame Guide)
3. Bring a **Gas Mask**: the World Tree interior has radiation zones that drain HP over time
4. Multi-element damage (Fire/Ground/Dragon/Electric) to cover each tower boss's weakness
5. Fast flying mount (Jetragon best) to dodge boss skills
