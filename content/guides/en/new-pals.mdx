## Featured New Pals

### Panthalus
- Element: Water
- Work Suitability: no confirmed work suitability
- Role: special Pal, possibly tied to World Tree story
- Source: high-frequency appearance in keywords

### Eidrolon / Eidrolon Ignis
- Element: Dragon/Dark and Dragon/Fire
- Work Suitability: Transporting
- Two forms: normal Eidrolon and fire variant Eidrolon Ignis

### Sekhmet
- Element: Ground
- Work Suitability: Handiwork, Mining, Transporting
- Role: versatile base worker

### Solenne
- Element: Dark/Neutral
- Work Suitability: Handiwork, Gathering, Transporting
- Role: Dark worker Pal

### Aegidron
- Element: Dragon/Ground
- Work Suitability: Mining
- Role: Dragon mining Pal

### Silvance
- Element: Grass
- Work Suitability: Planting, Handiwork, Medicine, Gathering, Transporting (5 work types)
- Role: one of the most versatile base workers

### Dandilord
- Element: Grass/Dark
- Work Suitability: Planting, Handiwork, Medicine, Gathering, Transporting (5 work types)
- Role: multi-purpose base worker

> These new Pals mainly appear in the World Tree and 1.0 endgame areas.
