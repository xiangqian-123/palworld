The Palworld 1.0 full release raised the level cap to 80, with the World Tree as the main-story endpoint. This guide covers the endgame main quest, core materials, legendary gear, repair costs and Hard Mode to take you from "beating the game" to "fully geared."

## Main-Story Finale: World Tree

The giant tree you could see from afar since EA launch but couldn't enter — the 1.0 full release opens it, and it's the end of the main story.

- **8 Towers + 8 Key Spheres** together seal the "Calamity" deep within the World Tree
- You must defeat each faction's tower boss before you can enter the World Tree
- Inside the World Tree drop World Tree Holy Water, Mythical Wood and other endgame materials

## Endgame Core Materials

| Material | How to Get | Main Use |
|------|---------|---------|
| World Tree Holy Water | World Tree dungeon drops | Ultimate potions, Paloxite ingot |
| Mythical Wood | Ancient giant tree / ancient relic reclaim | Ultimate gear |
| Soralite | Mine at Soralite Quarry (endgame zone) | Soralite ingot (many downstream recipes) |
| Paloxite | Ancient relic reclaim | Paloxite ingot (ultimate gear) |
| Chromite | Ancient material synthesizer / kill Silvegis | Hexolite (late-game weapon) |
| Coralum Ore | Underwater salvage / oil platform chests | Coral ingot (underwater gear) |
| Ancient Civilization Core | Boss battle drops | AI Core and other ultimate tech |

> Tip: Stock up on Hardwood and Ancient Civilization Parts early — they're in highest demand late.

## Legendary Schematics

Legendary schematics are the core of endgame gear, obtained mainly by:

- Defeating Alpha Pals (fixed-respawn large Pals) has a chance to drop the corresponding legendary schematic
- While killing/catching Alpha Pals, also collect "valuable" items to sell (see [Money Guide](/en/guide/money))

## Equipment Repair Costs (the most overlooked endgame pitfall)

Endgame weapon repair costs rise **dramatically**. Take the Rocket Launcher as an example: a single repair needs large amounts of Pal Metal Ingot and Polymer, far above mid-game weapons.

> Community feedback: endgame weapons' "punishing repair costs" is a frequent complaint. The fix is to keep the mine auto-producing the materials needed for Pal Metal Ingot, rather than mining on demand when it breaks.

- **Pal Metal Ingot**: crafted from Ore + Paldium Fragment
- **Polymer**: 2 High Quality Pal Oil + 1 Sulfur

## Hard Mode Bosses

After defeating 6 tower bosses, Hard Mode unlocks:

- Hard Mode bosses have broadly increased level, HP and damage
- Drop higher-tier legendary schematics and materials
- Require a fully geared Pal team + elemental advantage + top-tier gear

## Endgame Loadout Strategy

| Slot | Direction |
|------|------|
| Weapon | Legendary schematic weapon (high stats) |
| Armor | High defense + elemental resistance |
| Combat Pal | Elemental advantage + Rank 4 passives (Legend + Musclehead, etc.) |
| Mount | High-speed flying mount (travel/flanking) |
