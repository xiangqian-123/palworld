The Palworld 1.0 full release raised the level cap to 80, with the World Tree as the main-story endpoint. This guide covers the endgame main quest, core materials, legendary gear, repair costs and Hard Mode to take you from "beating the game" to "fully geared."

## Endgame Checklist

Work through this list top to bottom — each step anchors to its section below:

1. Clear all 8 towers and finish the [World Tree finale](/en/guide/world-tree)
2. Farm Radiant Gems in the World Tree and Awaken your core team (see the [World Tree guide](/en/guide/world-tree))
3. Unlock Hard Mode towers and farm legendary schematics (see [Legendary Schematics](#legendary-schematics))
4. Craft the gear at the Ancient Workbench (schematic must be in your inventory)
5. Buy the extra accessory slots and equip emblems + rings (see [Accessories](#accessories))
6. Automate materials: Soralite Quarry (Lv72) + Ancient Material Synthesiser (Lv78)
7. Protect your World Tree gathering with Holy Water or a World Tree passive Pal
8. Clear the Hard Mode towers, then Hard Zenara & Astralym

## Main-Story Finale: World Tree

The giant tree you could see from afar since EA launch but couldn't enter — the 1.0 full release opens it, and it's the end of the main story.

- **8 Towers + 8 Key Spheres** together seal the "Calamity" deep within the World Tree
- You must defeat each faction's tower boss before you can enter the World Tree
- Inside the World Tree drop World Tree Holy Water, Mythical Wood and other endgame materials

## Endgame Core Materials

| Material | How to Get | Main Use |
|------|---------|---------|
| World Tree Holy Water | World Tree drops | Awakening crystals, Paloxite ingot, gathering protection |
| Mythical Wood | World Tree / ancient relic reclaim | Ultimate gear |
| Soralite | **Sunreach only** — mine nodes with the Plasma Multicutter (Lv54), or automate with the Soralite Quarry (Lv72) | Soralite ingot (many downstream recipes) |
| Paloxite | **World Tree only** — or via the Ancient Relic Recycler (Lv74) | Paloxite ingot (ultimate gear) |
| Chromite | **Feybreak only**, buried underground — needs a Metal Detector (Lv56) or Smokie's partner skill | Hexolite (late-game weapons) |
| Coralum Ore | Underwater salvage / oil platform chests | Coral ingot (underwater gear) |
| Ancient Civilization Core | Boss battle drops | AI Core and other ultimate tech |

**AI Core** (unlocks at Lv67): 5 Computer + 10 Soralite Ingot + 2 Thermal Core + 1 Ancient Civilization Core.
**Paloxite Ingot** (Lv74, Ancient Furnace): 2 Paloxite + 1 Soralite + 1 World Tree Holy Water.

> **World Tree materials vanish on approach** — Paloxite, Mythical Wood and similar resources disappear if you get close unprotected. Guard against it by drinking World Tree Holy Water (a short buff) or keeping a Pal with a World Tree passive (God of Destruction / Hermit Sage / Dimensional Leap, etc.) in your party for permanent protection. Your mount needs protection too.

> Tip: Stock up on Hardwood and Ancient Civilization Parts early — they're in highest demand late.

## Legendary Schematics

Legendary schematics (Schematic 4) are the core of endgame gear. The reliable sources are the **Hard Mode tower bosses** — each drops its own schematic at about a 10% rate (per first-clear reports):

| Schematic | Source |
|---|---|
| Legendary Beam Launcher | Auri & Shaolong (Hard, Lv80) |
| Legendary Plasma Rifle | Saya & Selyne (Hard, Lv80) |
| Legendary Drone Launcher | Lily & Lyleen (Hard, Lv74) |
| Legendary Beam Scatter | Zoe & Grizzbolt (Hard, Lv72) |
| Legendary Lightweight Ancient Armor | Bjorn & Bastigor (Hard, Lv80) — 1092 defense, 3120 HP |

Chest-and-fishing pool (no boss source): the **Legendary Laser Sword** (2,400 attack, strongest melee) and **Legendary Mechanical Bow** come from Sunreach, Sakurajima and World Tree chests plus the Sunreach fishing route.

Craft the gear at the **Ancient Workbench** — keep the schematic in your inventory, not a storage box. Materials are Paloxite Ingot, Bio Battery, AI Cores, Ancient Civilization Cores and Ancient Civilization Parts.

## Accessories

Accessory slots start at 2 and cap at 4: buy the two upgrades from the Medal Merchant (the robed vendor) for Dog Coins — 50 + 300 in 1.0. The strongest 1.0 accessories:

| Accessory | Effect | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Dogen Emblem | Attack Up Lv4 (player + Pal) | Sunreach |
| Silvegis Emblem | Defense Up Lv4 (player + Pal) | Feybreak cave |
| Warsect Terra's Belt | Defense Up + Health Up Lv4 | Sakurajima |
| Vanguard Charm | Heat Res Lv3 + Cold Res Lv3 + Health Up Lv4 | Feybreak hacker terminal |
| Air Walker EX | Jump +2, Aerial Dash +2 | Sunreach ruins |
| Growth Acceleration Belt | Party-wide XP bonus | Mount Flopie |
| Phantom Ring | Extends dodge i-frames | Astral Mountain |
| Pal Tamer's Glasses | Mercy Hit + IV viewer in one slot | — |

Same-family accessories stack — for example two elemental-damage rings stack their bonuses, and a dual-element Pal benefits from two Batons.

## Equipment Repair Costs (the most overlooked endgame pitfall)

Endgame weapon repair costs rise **dramatically**. Take the Rocket Launcher as an example: a single repair needs large amounts of Pal Metal Ingot and Polymer, far above mid-game weapons.

> Community feedback: endgame weapons' "punishing repair costs" is a frequent complaint. The fix is to keep the mine auto-producing the materials needed for Pal Metal Ingot, rather than mining on demand when it breaks.

- **Pal Metal Ingot**: crafted from Ore + Paldium Fragment
- **Polymer**: 2 High Quality Pal Oil + 1 Sulfur

## Hard Mode Bosses

After you clear the 8 standard towers and defeat Normal Zenara & Astralym, Hard Mode opens — no world-settings change needed:

- Every Hard tower fight is a **raid-style encounter** with a Pal Box in the arena: field a full summon army (15–50 Pals), your own damage is halved and damage taken is up
- Hard bosses have broadly increased level, HP and damage, and drop the legendary schematics listed above
- Hard Zenara & Astralym is Lv100 with 5,030,099 HP — see the [World Tree guide](/en/guide/world-tree) for the wing-interrupt mechanic
- First-clear rewards include the Zenara Hat and the Psycho Gravity dark skill fruit (power 700)

## Endgame Base Locations

The 1.0 default is 4 bases (up to 10 in world settings). Popular endgame picks:

1. **Sunreach Soralite plateau** (≈ -305,-1410) — the only Soralite region in the game; with a high-level mining Pal your base auto-mines 6 nodes instead of hand-mining with the Plasma Multicutter.
2. **Unraidable highlands** (ore/coal at 188,-37; quartz at -210,250) — raiders need a walkable path, so plateau bases with cliff edges effectively never get raided.
3. **Oil bases** (Sunlit Isle ≈ 398,-465 / Oasis Island ≈ 900,191 / Sakurajima Oil Works -645,272) — 3 oil nodes in reach, easy to defend on water.
4. **Hexolite / Chromite outpost** (≈ -1180,-1230) — Hexolite is the frame of top-tier gear; Chromite can't be auto-mined by Pals, so run it as a personal outpost with a Metal Detector.
5. **World Tree shoreline / water base** (Aquatic Construction Kit, Lv66) — flat water plus jungle; a scenic endgame HQ. Note there is no "Holy Water base" — Holy Water is a consumable gathered at Teafant Springs.
