## Main-Story Finale: World Tree

The giant tree you could see from afar since EA launch but couldn't enter. The 1.0 full release opens it, and it's the end of the main story:

- **8 Towers + 8 Key Spheres** together seal the "Calamity" deep within the World Tree
- The story involves characters like Aurie, Zenara, Astralium, Pantalus (the last guardian whale)
- You must defeat each faction's tower boss before you can enter the World Tree
- Inside the World Tree drop World Tree Holy Water, Mythical Wood and other endgame materials

## Factions and Tower Boss Distribution

The Palpagos Islands have 7 major factions, each controlling a tower:

| Faction | Leader | Stronghold |
|------|------|------|

| Free Pal Alliance | Lily Everhart | Crescent Moon Shore / Verdant Brook |
| Brothers of the Eternal Pyre | Axel Travers | Mount Obsidian |
| Palpagos Islands Defense Force (PIDF) | Marcus Dryden | Settlements / Wildlife Sanctuaries |
| PAL Genetic Research Unit | Victor Ashford | Astral Mountains |
| Moonflower Clan | Saya Kurosaki | Sakurajima |
| Feybreak Warriors | Bjorn Seligsson | Feybreak |

## Exploration Tips

1. **Early**: Level to 15 at the Windswept Hills spawn point; don't enter volcano/snow/desert too early
2. **Mid**: Explore distant biomes after unlocking a flying mount
3. **Late**: Enter the endgame areas around the World Tree, mine Soralite (via Soralite Quarry) and other endgame materials
4. **Endgame**: Defeat each tower boss → enter the World Tree to challenge the Calamity

## Boss Distribution

Palworld has several boss types:

| Type | Description |
|------|------|
| Faction Leaders (Tower Bosses) | 7 faction leaders, each with a partner Pal, 10-minute time limit; defeating 6 unlocks Hard Mode |
| Raid Bosses | Powerful Pals summoned at your base, requiring defense prep |
| Bounty Targets | Named human enemies, drop Successful Bounty Token |
| Alpha Pals | Fixed-respawn large Pals |
| Predator Pals | Berserk Pals added in 0.4.11 |
