Palworld is a monster-taming open-world survival crafting game developed by Pocketpair. The full 1.0 version launched on July 10, 2026, leaving Early Access behind. This guide takes you from zero: catch Pals, build bases, set up automation and fight bosses.

## First 10 Minutes

1. **Spawn point selection**: Pick the starting plateau Grasslands area — rich resources and low-level enemies.
2. **Catch your first Pals**: Use your starting Pal Sphere to catch Lamball, Cattiva and Chikipi — these are basic resource-producing Pals.
3. **Build your first base**: Use Wood and Stone to set up a simple base and place a Palbox.
4. **Unlock the tech tree**: Press L to open the tech menu; prioritize unlocking Pal Sphere and Workbench.

> Tip: Don't rush to explore distant areas at the start — level up to 10 near your spawn point before setting out.

## Five Player Attributes

When leveling up, spend skill points on five core attributes that affect combat, survival, mobility and work efficiency:

| Attribute | Base | Per Level | Cap |
|------|-------|---------|------|
| HP | 100 | +100 | 5000 |
| Stamina | 100 | +10 | 600 |
| Attack | 100 | +2 | 200 |
| Work Speed | 100 | +50 | 2600 |
| Weight | 300 | +50 | 2800 |

> Later you can reset points (with items like Memory Wiping Medicine); early on feel free to put points into Weight and Work Speed first.

## Basic Construction

| Building | Unlock Level | Core Materials | Function |
|------|---------|---------|------|
| Palbox | 2 | 10 Paldium Fragment | Base core, holds 15–30 Pals |
| Workbench | 2 | 10 Wood | Craft tools and Pal Spheres |
| Logging Site | 7 | 50 Wood | Auto logging |
| Stone Pit | 7 | 50 Stone | Auto quarrying |
| Ranch | 7 | 50 Wood + 20 Stone | Pal produces materials (wool, eggs, milk, etc.) |
| Breeding Farm | 19 | 100 Wood + 20 Stone + 50 Fiber | Breed Pals |

## Resource Gathering Priority

- **Early**: Wood, Stone, Fiber, Paldium Fragment
- **Mid**: Ore, Coal, Sulfur, Leather
- **Late**: Hardwood, Pure Quartz, Crude Oil
- **Endgame**: Chromite, Coralum Ore, Soralite, Paloxite

## Breeding Basics

Breeding is the core way to obtain strong Pals, unlocked at level 19.

1. **Build a Breeding Farm**: 100 Wood + 20 Stone + 50 Fiber
2. **Make a Cake**: 5 Flour + 8 Red Berries + 7 Milk + 7 Eggs + 2 Honey
3. **Choose a pair**: Place 1 male + 1 female Pal + a Cake
4. **Hatch eggs**: Use an Egg Incubator, mind the temperature match

> Breeding formula: offspring BPV = FLOOR((father BPV + mother BPV + 1) / 2). Use [Breeding Calculator](/en/breeding-calculator) to look up combinations.

## Boss-Fight Prep

1. **Party setup**: At least 1 combat Pal (e.g. Grizzbolt / Orserk) + 1 mount (e.g. Fenglope / Nitewing) + 1 healing Pal
2. **Weapon upgrade**: Prepare a Crossbow or Single-Shot Sphere Launcher
3. **Medicine**: Bring Low Grade Medical Supplies
4. **Gear**: At least Leather Armor

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Too many base Pals causing lag**: The Palbox capacity is limited early on; don't hoard.
2. **Forgetting the cake in breeding**: Pals won't breed without a cake.
3. **Wrong egg incubation temperature**: Ice eggs go in cold zones, fire eggs in hot zones, normal eggs anywhere.
4. **No lights at night**: Hostile Pals appear at night; place a Campfire.

> Tip: The 1.0 full release has a complete storyline connecting 8 towers and the World Tree sealing arc — it's recommended to follow the main quest.
