The Palworld x Terraria crossover update "Tides of Terraria" launched in late June / early July 2025 (v0.6.0) — the largest crossover content Palworld has had, pushing Palworld back into Steam's Top 10 concurrent players the week it launched.

## Crossover Content Overview

| Content | Count / Description |
|------|----------|
| New Pals | 27 (including Terraria creatures) |
| New Islands | 3 (tropical / iceberg / shipwreck) + other small islands |
| New Bosses | Eye of Cthulhu (capturable) / Moon Lord (raid) |
| Terraria Dungeon | 1 large dungeon |
| New Weapons | Terra Blade / Meowmere etc., 6+ |
| New Armor | Hallowed Armor / Moon Lord Mask etc., 7+ |
| New Mechanics | Fishing / Salvage / Pal Trust / Arena |

## Terraria Dungeon (Sealed Realm of Terraria)

The **only fixed dungeon** added in v0.6.0, unlike other randomly spawning/rotating dungeons — fixed location and layout (coordinates roughly -422, -795):

- Contains Terraria-exclusive enemies and bosses (some capturable)
- Drops Terraria-exclusive weapons, items and **exclusive Schematics**
- **Two fixed boss fights**
- Enterable at any level, but **level 45+ recommended**
- After defeating the final boss, get the **Celestial Sigil** from the final chest, unlocking an extra challenge

> Compared to normal dungeons: normal dungeons disappear after ~200 minutes and relocate, while the Terraria dungeon is permanently fixed.

## Raid Bosses

### Eye of Cthulhu
- Classic Terraria boss, capturable as a Pal in Palworld
- Drops high-tier loot
- First phase accompanied by Cave Bats

### Moon Lord
- Ultimate raid boss with massive HP
- Requires team coordination and top-tier gear
- Not capturable

## New Mechanics

### Fishing
- Craft a Fishing Rod
- Catch Water Pals and seabed resources
- Build a Fishing Pond at base

### Salvaging
- Ride a Water Pal out to sea
- Use a rod to salvage resources from the seabed
- Obtain Coralum Ore, etc.

### Pal Trust
- Interact with Pals to raise trust
- High trust = stat bonus
- Can rest beside a Pal

### Arena
- Single-player challenge mode
- Battle warriors across the Palpagos Islands
- Win Battle Tickets to redeem rewards
- Multiplayer mode has spectator function
