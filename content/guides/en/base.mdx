The base is the core of Palworld — capturing, production, breeding and crafting all revolve around it. A well-planned base produces resources fully automatically; a poorly planned one gets raided daily and its Pals go on strike. This guide covers the whole flow from location to automation.

## Base Location (the most important step)

Where you place your base decides resource efficiency. Check what's nearby before choosing:

| Consideration | Suggestion |
|------|------|
| Terrain | Flat open ground, easy to place buildings |
| Ore | Nearby Ore node (a late-game necessity) |
| Water | Close to water for irrigation |
| Safety | Avoid high-level hostile Pal areas |

> Tip: One base early on; open multiple specialized bases later — one for mining, one for logging, one for breeding — far more efficient than a single "all-purpose base."

## Palbox and Base Radius

The Palbox is the core of your base, unlocked at level 2, holding 15–30 Pals. **The Palbox's coverage area is the base radius** — this is also the root of the community complaint "the base is too small," so check the range before placing buildings.

- Upgrading the Palbox expands capacity and work slots
- Too many base Pals cause lag; don't hoard

## SAN Management and Pal Status

Low SAN (Sanity) makes Pals work slower, get sick and eventually go on strike. Keep SAN up with three things:

1. **Beds**: Provide enough beds
2. **Food**: Keep the feed box full; don't run out
3. **Hot Spring**: Let Pals recover sanity

> Tip: Low SAN is the most common cause of "Pals not working" — check beds, food and hot spring first before swapping Pals.

## Base Defense

Hostile factions or wild Pals raid your base periodically:

- Build walls + leave 1–2 gaps (choke point) to bunch up enemies
- Place defense structures at the gaps (ballista, machine gun tower)
- Station combat-type Pals to defend

## Efficient Layout Tips

- **Vertical stacking**: Build upward to save space vs. spreading out horizontally
- **Storage near production**: Keep storage next to work stations to cut Pal transport time
- **Specialized bases**: Build mining, logging and breeding separately; don't mix

## Common Beginner Mistakes (high-frequency community pitfalls)

1. Too many work stations but not enough workers
2. Catching Pals but not assigning work manually (they won't auto-start)
3. No ore node near the location, forcing a move later
4. Ignoring Pal happiness (hunger, no bed, no hot spring)
5. Storage too far from production stations

> Fixing any 3 of the above doubles base output.
