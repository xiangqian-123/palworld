Breeding is the core system for obtaining strong Pals in Palworld. This guide details breeding formulas, the calculator and the best combos.

## Breeding Prerequisites

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Unlock Level | Level 19 |
| Building | Breeding Farm |
| Build Materials | 100 Wood + 20 Stone + 50 Fiber |
| Required | 1 Male Pal + 1 Female Pal + 1 Cake |
| Egg Incubator | Unlocked at Lv10: 10 Paldium Fragment + 5 Cloth + 30 Stone + 2 Ancient Civilization Parts |

## Cake Recipe

Cake is required for breeding, made in a Cooking Pot:

| Material | Amount | Source |
|------|------|------|
| Flour | 5 | Mill (3 Wheat → 1 Flour) |
| Red Berries | 8 | Wild gather / Caprity drops |
| Milk | 7 | Mozzarina ranch output |
| Eggs | 7 | Chikipi ranch output |
| Honey | 2 | Beegarde ranch output |

> Automated cake line: grow Wheat (harvested by Lifmunk/Lyleen) → Chikipi lays eggs → Mozzarina gives milk → Beegarde gives honey → Kindling Pal auto-cooks.

## Breeding Formula (Breeding Rank)

Each Pal species has a hidden Breeding Rank value; lower is rarer and stronger. Offspring level takes the average of the parents' ranks rounded:

```
Offspring Rank = ⌊(Father Rank + Mother Rank + 1) / 2⌋
```

- The offspring is the species whose Rank is closest to that value (if two species are equally distant, the one with the smaller index is chosen)
- The result is usually **deterministic**: the same parents (same species + gender combo) always produce the same thing
- A few species don't use the Rank system and instead use **fixed combos** (see "Variants and Special Combos" below)
- There's also a 1% chance (3% with Extravagant Vegetable Cake) of producing a **variant egg** that hatches a rarer Pal

## Pals That Can Only Breed Same-Species

The following Pals **can only** be bred with two same-species parents and cannot be obtained through cross-species combos:

Grizzbolt, Lyleen, Orserk, Faleris, Shadowbeak, Selyne, Bastigor, Blazamut Ryu, Paladius, Necromus, Frostallion, Jetragon, Bellanoir Libero, Mimog, Xenovader, Xenogard, Xenolord

> Note: "Legendary" Pals like Orserk and Shadowbeak are NOT producible from normal cross-species combos — many old guides got this wrong.

## Passive Skill Inheritance

Offspring can inherit passives from both parents; the rules are more complex than they seem:

- Each Pal has 0–4 passive slots
- Inheritance flow: merge both parents' passives (dedupe) → roll a 1–4 "inheritance count" (probabilities 40%/30%/20%/10%) → then roll a "mutation count" to add random passives
- **Identical passives on both parents neither help nor hurt the odds** (counted once after dedupe)
- Probability of inheriting all parent passives: 1 → 40%, 2 → 24%, 3 → 12%, 4 → 10%
- **Best practice**: Have the parents total only 4 target passives (2+2 / 3+1 / 4+0) to avoid extra random passives
- Legendary passives (Legend, elemental Emperor) cannot mutate randomly and must be inherited from parents
- Active skills: 50% chance to inherit 1 from parents (including unequipped skills)

Best combat passive combo: **Legend + Musclehead + Serenity + 30% elemental passive** (Serenity has replaced the old Ferocious/Brave combo)

See [Passive Mechanics Explained](/en/guide/passives) for details.

## Variant Eggs and Special Cakes

| Cake Type | Effect |
|----------|------|
| Normal Cake | Standard breeding |
| Extravagant Vegetable Cake | Variant rate up from 1% to 3% |

Variant-egg Pals are always Alpha, always carry 4 passives (at least 2 T4 passives, including 1 variant-exclusive passive), and come with 2 levels of condensation. Variant-exclusive passives include: Babysitter, Heavily Armored, Idiosyncratic, Immortality, Skymarcher.

## Gender Ratio and Alpha

- Most Pals are 1:1; few exceptions (e.g. Kingpaca 90% male, Lovander 30% male, Beegarde/Elizabee 10% male, Bellanoir 5% male)
- Bred Pals have a 5% chance to be Alpha (unrelated to parents, not inherited)

## FAQ

### Q: Do Pals evolve?
No. Palworld has no evolution system. Variants are independent species that must be obtained separately.

### Q: Breeding is too slow, what do I do?
Adjust the Egg Incubation Speed multiplier in World Settings. Dedicated servers edit PalWorldSettings.ini.

### Q: Can same-sex Pals breed?
No. You need 1 male and 1 female.
