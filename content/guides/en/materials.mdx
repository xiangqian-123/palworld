Palworld 1.0 has **149 crafting materials** (excluding Pal eggs and gold), split into 14 categories. This page quick-checks how to get and use each material by category.

## Mining Ores and Stone (20)

### Ores with World Nodes

| Material | Map Nodes | Clustered Areas | Main Use |
|------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Stone | 8,157 rocks + 4,286 small rocks | All regions | Foundation, cement, early buildings |
| Wood | 4,654 | Forest | Charcoal, plank, walls |
| Ore | 1,632 | Grassland & desert cliffs | Iron ingot, refined ingot, plasteel |
| Coal | 824 | Desert & Obsidian volcano | Refined ingot, thermal core, gunpowder |
| Paldium Fragment | 711 | Blue crystal veins in all regions | Pal Sphere, Pal Terminal |
| Sulfur | 655 | Desert & volcano | Ammo, polymer, corrosive solvent |
| Hexolite Quartz | 635 | Feybreak Island | Hexolite (late-game firearms) |
| Pure Quartz | 607 | Snowfields & Sakurajima | Circuit board, Soralite ingot |
| Nightstar Sand | 271 | Sakurajima | Skystrider & sprint boots recipes |
| Soralite | 226 | Sunrise Island only | Soralite ingot (95 downstream recipes) |
| Beautiful Flower | 27 | Scattered | Charm, baton, Memory Wiping Medicine |
| Ancient Lava | 10 | Volcano ground (rarest node) | Skystrider Mk II, Ring of Trust |

### Ores without Nodes (special acquisition)

| Material | Best Source | Yield |
|------|-------------|------|
| Chromite | Ancient Material Synthesizer (8,000 work each) | — |
| Coralum Ore | Salvage debris (level 2) / oil platform chests | 6-11 per pull |

| Crude Oil | Large oil platform chests | 10-300 per chest |
| Meteorite Fragment | Kill Xenolord | 7-15 per kill |

## Ancient Relics (5)

Recycler core loop: recycling relics yields Paloxite + Mythical Wood + a chance of Ancient Civilization Core.

| Relic | Kill Yield | Recycle Output | Core Chance |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Decayed Ancient Relic | 30-50 | 1-3 Paloxite + 1-3 Mythical Wood | 20% |
| Dormant Ancient Relic | 10-20 | 3-5 + 3-5 | 24% |
| Gorgeous Ancient Relic | 4-8 | 5-7 + 5-7 | 28.8% |
| Glowing Ancient Relic | 2-3 | 7-9 + 7-9 | 34.6% |
| Glistening Ancient Relic | 1 | 9-11 + 9-11 | 41.5% |
