Palworld, developed by Pocketpair, launched in Steam Early Access on January 19, 2024 and released its full 1.0 version on July 10, 2026. This page collects all purchase channels, pricing and system requirements.

## Purchase Channels

| Platform | Price | Notes |
|------|------|------|
| Steam (PC) | $29.99 | Limited-time 25% off ≈ $22.49 |
| Mac App Store | $29.99 | Native Apple Silicon support |
| Xbox Store (Series X|S / One) | $29.99 | Supports Xbox One |
| PlayStation Store (PS5) | $29.99 | No PS4 version |
| Xbox Game Pass | $16.99/mo (Ultimate) | Includes PC + console + cloud gaming |

> The official 1.0 full release confirmed no price increase, keeping the EA pricing.

## Steam Page

Buy directly: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1623730/Palworld/

## Cross-Platform Multiplayer

| Platform Combo | Supported |
|----------|---------|
| PC ↔ Xbox | Yes |
| PC ↔ PS5 | Yes |
| Xbox ↔ PS5 | Yes |
| Mac ↔ All Platforms | Yes |
| Nintendo Switch | Not supported (no Switch version) |
| PS4 | Not supported (no PS4 version) |

Cross-platform multiplayer has been supported since v0.5.0 (March 19, 2025). The host enables Cross-Play in World Settings.

| Item | Minimum | Recommended |

## Mac Version

The Mac version is purchased on the Mac App Store and supports native Apple Silicon. The 1.0 full release launched simultaneously with cross-platform multiplayer support.
