Fishing is a system added in Palworld's v0.6.0 (Tides of Terraria) update. This page aggregates all fishing data: tech unlocks, wild fishing spot tiers, 78 fishable Pals, base fishing pond catch rates, salvage drop tables, baits and fishing Pals.

## Fishing Prerequisite: Tech Unlock

All rods, baits and ponds are **tech unlocks** with no world drops. A rod alone can't cast — you must also have bait in your inventory.

| Name | Tech Level | Points | Notes |

| High Quality Bait | Lv 22 | 1 | — |

## Wild Fishing Spots: 6 Tiers

Each wild fishing spot is marked with one of six tiers; the tier decides: shadow combat difficulty, the level range of biting Pals, and the rod durability consumed per cast.

| Spot Tier | Combat Difficulty | Capture Level | Durability per Cast | Pals in Pool |
|---------|---------|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Beginner | 15–78 | Lv 1–28 | 1 | 15 Pals |
| Intermediate | 38–87 | Lv 20–45 | 1/2/4 | 30 Pals |
| Advanced | 52–96 | Lv 30–69 | 2/4 | 22 Pals |
| Expert | 52–106 | Lv 40–69 | 4 | 28 Pals |
| Master | 84–106 | Lv 65–69 | 4 | 4 Pals |
| Grand Master | 104–130 | Lv 71–79 | 4 | 4 Pals |

> At the bottom tier, each cast costs 1 durability; from Advanced onward each cast costs 4 — the same rod can only cast 1/4 as many times at the top tier. Bring a backup rod before heading into deep water.

## Base Fishing Pond

Both pond types (Fishing Pond, Large Fishing Pond) are **production buildings** that need a **Handiwork Pal** assigned; once built they fish automatically — the only way to grind odds without manually casting.

- The Large Pond unlocks later and can fish the largest shadows at home
- A pond **can't take a second worker**; slot cap is 1, and Handiwork level 1 is enough
- **Placement restricted**: only within the base camp radius, can't be built by a lake
- The Large Pond needs 85,000 work (small pond 23,500), one of the heaviest buildings in the game
- Ponds **don't consume SAN** (like ranches, breeding farms, oil pumps), but have no manual mode — assign no worker and they do nothing

### Catch Rate (by shadow size)

| Shadow | Top-Tier Normal Catch |
|------|-------------|
| Small | Killamari / Jelliette / Turtacle / Jellroy / Celaray / Pengullet / Kelpsea / Croajiro / Fuack at 9.5% each |
| Medium | Gloopie 10%, Celaray Lux 9.5%, Surfent / Dumud at 9.5% each |
| Large | Azurobe Cryst 11%, Turtacle Terra 10.1%, Croajiro Noct / Penking Lux / Whalaska / Whalaska Ignis / Ghangler Ignis at 7.3% each |

> Boss-size versions have far lower catch rates than normal (mostly 0.2%–0.5%), making them rare jackpots.

### Three things worth grinding specifically

1. **Rod schematics**: Ponds can drop a schematic one tier above what you own. High-tier schematics only appear in the Azurobe Cryst / Finsider Ignis / Ghangler Ignis / Whalaska table, roughly **one per 440 large-shadow catches**.
2. **Ancient tech**: Sun Sphere, Ancient Sphere, Ancient Civilization Core and Old Pal Manuscript only appear in the large-shadow Skutlass/Solmora table and Whalaska Ignis table. Small/medium shadows drop none.
3. **Coralum Ore**: From Munchill/Polapup table at 1–3, the four large tables stack increasing counts (2–4 → 8–18 → 12–21 → 18–27). The Whalaska Ignis table also has the highest gold roll in the whole pool (7,000–7,500).

## Salvage (magnet fishing)

Casting at **floating debris** in open water won't get a bite — instead it hooks a junk box. No shadow, no size target, bait doesn't participate. What's consumed is the **magnet**; which magnet you carry decides which table you hit.

| Magnet | Cost |
|------|------|
| Normal Fishing Magnet | 1 Iron Ingot makes 10, no tech requirement |
| Powerful Fishing Magnet | Tech Lv 62, 1 Hexolite + 1 Low-Temp Coolant + 1 Bio Battery makes 10 |

### Shallow vs Deep Water (core difference)

| | Shallow (Normal Magnet) | Deep (Powerful Magnet) |
|---|---|---|
| Main Output | **Gold** (≈1,660 per cast) | **Dog Coins + Schematics + Manuals + Coral Ore** |
| Schematics | Beginner Rod schematic 1% | 24 weapon/Hexolite armor schematics (combined 12.4%) |
| Coral Ore | 1–2 (30% chance) | 6–11 (always drops) |
| Training Manuals | M/L small amounts | L/XL at 21.9% each |

> Deep-water salvage is one of the fastest Coralum Ore sources; shallow-water salvage is one of the most stable early gold incomes in the game.

## Bait Comparison

Baits aren't a straight upgrade — each changes different values. **Sweet Bait and High Quality Bait work identically.**

| Bait | Effect |
|------|------|
| Simple Bait | Baseline, no modifier |
| High Quality Bait | ×1.5 bite chance |
| Deluxe Bait | ×1.15 catch bar |
| Beginner Bait | ×0.68 gauge drain |
| Sweet Bait | ×1.5 bite chance (same as High Quality) |
| Lucky Bait | ×1.25 catch bar, +40% item and Pal drops |
| Quick Bait | ×1.5 bite chance, ×1.25 catch bar |
| Alluring Bait | ×1.3 catch bar, +40% item and Pal drops |
| Risky Bait | ×1.3 catch bar, +80% item and Pal drops (bar narrows) |

> Farming materials not Pals → **Risky Bait** (the only bait that doubles both drop rolls); just want to land catches → **Alluring Bait** (widest bar).

## 7 Fishing Pals (partner skills)

Seven Pals' partner skills **only work near water** (value range rank 1 → 5). These skills don't change what you catch, only whether you successfully reel it in.

| Pal | Skill | Effect |
|------|------|------|
| Jelliette | Jelliette Drop | Companion item drop +55% → +95% |
| Jellroy | Jellroy Drop | Salvage yield +55% → +95% |
| Solmora Lux | Shocking Fish | High-stat-potential Pal chance +74% → +82% |
| Solmora | Charming Fish | High-stat-potential Pal chance +70% → +80% |
| Gloopie | Sticky Princess | Gauge drain on bar deviation -12% → -35% |
| Whalaska Ignis | Cozy Whale Cruiser | Starting gauge partial fill +7% → +17%; aim fill +7% → +17% |
| Whalaska | Chilled Whale Cruiser | Starting gauge partial fill +5% → +14%; aim fill +5% → +14% |

### Choose by use

- **Keep high-quality Pals** → Solmora Lux (74→82% stat-potential bonus, the only skill that changes catch quality)
- **Farm materials** → Jelliette (max-level item and Pal drops +95% each, doubles again with Risky Bait)
- **Salvage debris** → Jellroy (bonus only applies to salvage)
- **Struggling with the minigame** → Whalaska Ignis (strictly better than Whalaska); Gloopie's 12→35% drain cut is the single biggest value and the earliest obtainable

> Only Solmora and Solmora Lux are water mounts; star rating rises via condensation (needs same-species duplicates) or Star Fruits (redeemed with Dog Coins at the medal shop).

## Fishing Tips

1. **Watch underwater shadows**: big shadow = big fish / rare Pal; Boss-size is a rare jackpot
2. **Bring a spare rod**: high-tier spots cost 4 durability per cast
3. **Clear the pond**: a pond won't accept new targets while a caught Pal sits uncollected; a full Palbox stops it
4. **Pair with fishing Pals**: Jelliette for materials, Solmora Lux for quality, Jellroy for salvage
